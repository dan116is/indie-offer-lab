## 2. 30-day grid

Fill dates for your next 30 days. Status: planned / posted / skipped. You may compress to 8-10 posts. Crossing off is success.

Pattern: Day 1 W, 2 rest, 3 L, 4 rest, 5 S, 6-7 off, then repeat with an Ask on weeks 2 and 4. Days 27-30: review week; skip leftover slots.

## 3. Prompt formulas

**W:** Here is [artifact] as of [date]. The ugly part is [x]. Not a tutorial.

**A:** If you already bought [SKU] or do this job: which step failed? Reply; I will not add you to a list.

## 4. Stop rules

If a slot slips 48 hours, mark skipped. Do not catch up with three posts in an hour. If you have nothing true to say, skip. Never buy comments. Never DM strangers thoughts on this? Never invent metrics in a ship note.
