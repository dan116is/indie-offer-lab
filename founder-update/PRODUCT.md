# Monthly Founder Update Template

**SKU:** `founder-update`  
**Price:** $9 USD, paid in USDC on Base  
**Format:** Markdown letter you send once a month  
**Audience:** US indie hackers writing to buyers, a tiny list, or a public log  
**Promise:** A readable monthly update that does not invent traction.

This folder may also keep a sibling weekly internal pack as `PRODUCT-weekly-internal.md`. This file is the **monthly** letter.

## Guardrails

- Past tense for things that happened. Future tense only for dated commitments.
- Money lines are copied from `metrics-weekly`, not from a goal spreadsheet.
- If net cash was `$0`, write `$0`.
- Do not include other people’s private emails, revenue, or screenshots.
- One ask at the end, or zero asks.
- Skip empty theater. A short honest letter beats a complete fake one.

## Header

```markdown
# [Product] — [Month Year] update
From: [your name], [US city or “US, remote”]
Period: [first day] → [last day]
```

## 1. One sentence

> This month I [shipped / did not ship] [thing], and [money or usage fact you can open in a tab].

Allowed: “I shipped v1.2 and collected three $9 payments.” / “I shipped nothing. I refunded one wrong-SKU order.”  
Disallowed: “momentum,” “going viral,” “on track for $10k.”
