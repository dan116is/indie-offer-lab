# Shareable Feature List — 25 Screenshot-able Prompts

**SKU:** `shareable-feature-list`  
**Price:** $9 USD, paid in USDC on Base  
**Audience:** US indie hackers who need a public feature list without fake dashboards

These are prompts for *your own product facts*. If a prompt needs a number you have not measured, skip it or label **unverified**. No fake MRR, no fake logos, no “as seen in.”

**How to shoot:** one idea per frame. Light background. Your domain in the corner. Square (1:1) or 4:5. Text large enough to read at 2 inches wide.

## Rules

1. Each card is a feature of the buyer’s experience, not a stack flex.
2. Prefer verbs the buyer does.
3. The screenshot must stand alone.
4. Do not screenshot other people’s UI.

## The 25 prompts

### 1. First win without an account
**Overlay:** Open `[PRODUCT]`. No signup. First page is the work.  
`[Subline: files, not a funnel.]`

### 2. Timed, not theatrical
**Overlay:** I timed the first pass: `[N]` minutes on a cold laptop.  
`[Subline: stopwatch or don’t print a number.]`

### 3. English that ships
**Overlay:** US English. Written to be pasted, not “workshopped.”

### 4. Blanks you cannot skip
**Overlay:** Fill-ins are in ALL CAPS so you cannot skim past the decisions.

### 5. One buyer, named
**Overlay:** Built for `[role]` who already has `[channel]`.  
`[Subline: not “anyone with a dream.”]`

### 6. Explicit skip list
**Overlay:** Skip `[PRODUCT]` if you need `[calls / ads / a lawyer]`.

### 7. Price is the feature
**Overlay:** `[PRICE]`. One time. No seat math.

### 8. Pay without a card network
**Overlay:** USDC on Base. Chain id 8453. Email the hash.  
`[Subline: no memo field on ERC-20 — slug goes in the email.]`

### 9. Delivery you can describe
**Overlay:** You get a folder: `index.html` + `PRODUCT.md`.

### 10. Printable
**Overlay:** Designed to survive paper. Checkboxes are real `☐`.

### 11. Worked example (labeled fiction if fiction)
**Overlay:** Includes a filled example. Fictional names are marked fictional.  
`[Subline: zero invented revenue.]`

### 12. Stop test
**Overlay:** A “you are done when” list so you stop researching.

### 13. Do-not-copy filter
**Overlay:** Leaves you with three things you will *not* take from a rival.

### 14. Refund sentence on the same page
**Overlay:** Defects: re-send or refund. Taste: read the headings first.

### 15. Not legal advice (when it matters)
**Overlay:** Snippets are labeled. Not a law firm. Not your counsel.

### 16. License in one screen
**Overlay:** Use in client work. Don’t resell the raw pack.

### 17. Support hours that exist
**Overlay:** Email, `[days]`, `[timezone]`. No Discord you would ignore.

### 18. Version date
**Overlay:** This version: `[YYYY-MM-DD]`. No fake roadmap.

### 19. Table of contents = file
**Overlay:** Every H2 on the landing page is an H2 in the file.

### 20. Ugly-edge callouts
**Overlay:** We tell you what is missing: `[no API / no team seats / no VAT calc]`.

### 21. Artifact, not vibe
**Overlay:** You leave with `[a filled grid / a terms paragraph / a 25-card list]`.

### 22. Single-thread checkout
**Overlay:** One address. One token. One orders inbox.

### 23. Same-day usable
**Overlay:** If you start after lunch, you can publish a paragraph before dinner.  
`[Subline: only post this if you timed it.]`

### 24. Screenshot of *your* filled page
**Overlay:** (This frame is a photo of your handwriting or a filled markdown.)

### 25. The anti-feature
**Overlay:** Will not: buy ads for you, promise MRR, or clone someone’s starter kit.
