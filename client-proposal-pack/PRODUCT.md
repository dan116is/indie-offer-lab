## Discovery (must hear before you quote)

1. What must be true in 90 days for this to have been worth paying for?
2. Who signs, who uses, who blocks?
3. What have you already tried?
4. Hard deadline and why that date is real.
5. Budget range they can approve without a new round — or “unknown, stop after discovery.”
6. Explicitly out of scope (ads, legal, translations, hosting, photography)?
7. Time zone and hours they can spend with you.
8. How they pay and in which currency.

Kill rule: if 1, 2, and 4 are empty after one reminder, do not write a custom proposal.

## One-page outline

```
[YOUR FIRM] · Proposal for [CLIENT] · [DATE]
Valid until [DATE + 14] · Currency [XXX] · Time zone [IANA]
1. Who (one sentence)
2. Situation (four lines, their words)
3. Outcome (observable; not “more presence”)
4. Options A / B / C (table)
5. Timeline in calendar weeks
6. You provide / we provide
7. Out of scope
8. Assumptions
9. Price, payment schedule, start rule
10. How to accept: reply “we accept option B” + pay invoice 1
```
