# 10 Testimonial Request Scripts

**SKU:** `testimonial-asks`  
**Price:** $9 USD, paid in USDC on Base  
**Format:** 10 copy-paste scripts + permission/edit workflow  
**Audience:** US indie hackers who have real users  
**Promise:** Ask for a usable quote without fake proof.

Do not buy reviews. Do not ghostwrite praise. Do not invent MRR in a quote. Append the permission line every time.

## Guardrails

- Ask people who **used** the thing.
- One ask per person. If ignored: wait 10 days, send script 9 once, then stop.
- Say what you will do with the words (site, X post).
- Cap: 8 asks per product version.

## Minimum viable testimonial

First name + role · what they used · before · after. No numbers they did not write.

**Permission line:** If you are willing, I will use your first name, role, and this quote on my product page. I will not add numbers you did not write. Reply “ok to publish” or “private only.”

## Script 1 — Same-day first win

**Subject:** one line I can quote (optional)

You just got [specific win]. If you have 90 seconds: what were you trying to do, and what happened after you used [product]? Two sentences is enough. [permission line]

## Script 2 — Day 8 of real use

**Subject:** quick check after a week of [product]

What job did it sit in? One sentence on the job + one on what you still do by hand. [permission line]

## Script 3 — They already posted

**Subject:** can I use your [post] on the page?

You already wrote: “[paste unedited]”. May I put that on the page with first name + role? Reply “ok to publish” or “please don’t.”

## Script 4 — Support thread that closed

**Subject:** glad that [issue] is unstuck

What did you try before you wrote me, and what should the docs have said on line one? I will quote that next to troubleshooting — not as a 5-star review. [permission line]

## Script 5 — Named job

**Subject:** how [product] sat in your [job]

Fill: Before I was ____. I used [product] to ____. After I could ____ without ____. No revenue numbers. [permission line]

## Script 6 — Short DM

Quick ask: you used [product] for [job]. Can I quote 1–2 sentences (first name + role)? Voice-note is fine; I will transcribe and send back for yes/no.

## Script 7 — Draft they can redline

**Subject:** I can draft, you can redline

Draft from **their** emails only. Do not publish until they reply edits or “ok as-is.” [permission line]

## Script 8 — Tight quote + identity

Need: 2 sentences max; first name; role; city or skip; a link you control or “no link.” [permission line]

## Script 9 — They said yes, file never arrived (once, 7–10 days)

You said yes to a quote for [product]. Reply with two sentences, “draft it from my last email,” or “close.”

## Script 10 — Screenshot + caption

Skip the quote. Send a screenshot of [artifact] with secrets boxed out + a caption in your words. No fake numbers. [permission line]
