- Count: 12 invoices left Slack the week of 2026-08-17 (buyer's count)

- Revenue you did not see
- Engagement with no definition

## 2. Interview (20 minutes)

Record only with consent. Paraphrase unless they approve a verbatim line.

1. What was the Tuesday problem in one story?
2. What did you try before us?
3. What almost made you not buy?
4. What did you actually do in the first hour?
5. What is still annoying?
6. Which sentence would you be willing to put your name under?
7. What should a peer *not* expect?

Capture **one** still-annoying item. That line is the honesty that makes the rest believable.
