## 0. Ground rules

- You are studying *buyer experience*, not scraping private data, not creating fake accounts against terms, not copying trademarked copy.
- Use only public pages: homepage, pricing, public changelog, public docs, public social posts.
- One primary competitor. Time-box: 90 minutes. Then decide.
- Output is three sentences you can paste into your landing page *in your own words*.

If you cannot describe the gap without using their headline adjectives, you are still copying.

# Example section (fictional products)

The following companies **do not exist**. There are **no revenue claims**. Not market data.

- **Your product (example):** QuietHour — a one-page markdown ritual for US solo founders who keep sliding “deep work” after Slack.
- **Rival (example):** DeskPing — a public marketing site for a browser nudge that pings every 25 minutes.
- **Buyer you can reach:** your own X/Twitter followers who already complain about calendar spray. No ads.

| Zone | Notice in 8 seconds | Guess |
|---|---|---|
| Title / H1 | Timer-centric (“never miss a focus block”) | Urgency |
| Subhead | Integrates with a generic calendar | Assumes calendar life |
| Primary button | “Start free” | Trial anxiety |
| Social proof | Three unnamed “makers” | Thin |
| Price visibility | Not on first screen | Suspect monthly |
| Not shown | Offline / print path | Tab-haters ignored |

Implied promise (paraphrase): the app will interrupt you into focus. QuietHour disagrees. Proof on page: none with units. Price in this fiction: $8/mo, no lifetime. Package: hosted account required.

| Step | Path | Time | Friction |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Land | Hero + Start free | 10s | Account |
| 2. Pay or start | Email signup | 2–5 min | Another inbox |
| 3. First artifact | Enable notifications | 5 min | Permission |
| 4. Know it worked | A ping happens | 25 min | Stay in the browser |

QuietHour first win in this fiction: fill four lines, set a kitchen timer. **Stopwatch: 7 minutes.** When you ship, use your date or write unverified.

| # | Gap | This week? | Proof |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Focus ritual that works if I close the laptop | yes | Photo of printed one-pager |
| 2 | I will not create an account for a $12 problem | yes | USDC or a file email |
| 3 | I want the rule in writing, not a ping | yes | The filled worksheet is the artifact |

Do-not-copy: (1) “never miss a ___ block” (2) tomato-timer visuals (3) “Start free” if you do not have a trial.

Three sentences QuietHour can ship:
1. **Who:** US solo founders who already know they will not install another focus app.
2. **Win:** A one-page ritual you can finish before your next meeting.
3. **Unlike them:** No account, no pings — you leave with a written rule and a kitchen timer.
