## How to use

1. Pick **one** refund posture (A, B, or C). Mixing “no refunds” with “14-day guarantee” on the same SKU confuses chargebacks.
2. Pair it with the matching license line and the delivery line.
3. Put the same text in three places: checkout/pay block, a `/terms` page, and the receipt email.
4. Replace `[BRACKETS]`. Do not ship brackets.

Assumes **$9 to $49**, **one-time** digital files, **no subscription**.
